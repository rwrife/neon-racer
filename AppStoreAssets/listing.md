## Description

Take the wheel in CyberRun, a neon arcade racer built for quick runs and one more try. Steer through glowing routes, time your boosts, and keep your speed through every turn.

Score points for distance, speed, overtakes, near misses, drifts, and checkpoints. Finish strong to improve your rank and unlock more vehicles, color palettes, and routes. Tune your ride in the garage, then head back out to beat your best score.

CyberRun is made for touch controls and plays offline. Your progress is saved on your device, with no account required.
