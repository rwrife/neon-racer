### Description

Race through a vivid neon course built for quick starts and focused runs. Neon Racer combines responsive arcade handling with a deterministic simulation, making every improvement yours to earn.

**Features**

- Immediate, touch-first arcade racing
- A bold retro-futurist presentation
- Fast restarts for score-chasing runs
- Local progress with no account required
- No ads, analytics, or tracking
- Offline play
